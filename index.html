How to read it. Each link pivots on its frame-side mounting point (the fixed end). As the suspension cycles up and down, the axle-side end swings along the faint arc of travel. The arcs now follow each link's real angle — tilt a bar and its arc tilts with it.

Why the arcs matter. If the track bar and drag link sweep the axle sideways by different amounts through the same wheel travel, the axle gets steered by bumps (bump steer). Match their lateral sweep — same length and angle relationship — to minimize it. The mismatch figure above is that difference over your chosen travel range.
